# Audience, editorial value, and sustainable return

## Business hypothesis

Readers return because Wrap Brief reduces repeated coverage, shows trustworthy sources, and offers useful original context. Revenue follows that repeat value. A low-cost headline directory alone has weak differentiation, uncertain search visibility, and limited purchase intent.

Primary objective: contribution after cash costs **and operator time**, with reader trust, publisher referrals, accessibility, and privacy as guardrails. Never sell ranking or hide the original publisher link to inflate page views.

## Stage 1: validate useful reading

- Clear daily positioning, 3–5 complementary approved sources, accurate groups, good mobile performance, honest freshness, prominent outbound links.
- Publish an original reviewed weekly briefing or selected explanatory context when operator capacity permits; no automated article paraphrasing.
- Quality original pages can be indexed; do not produce hundreds of thin keyword/tag/duplicate cluster pages. Use accurate canonical URLs and selective `noindex`/sitemap inclusion.
- Encourage source suggestions/corrections through public-safe contribution routes. Open source supports trust and contributions but is not a consumer acquisition plan by itself.
- Share selected useful summaries only where community rules allow. No automated promotional spam, scraped email outreach, or deceptive Nintendo affiliation.

## Stage 2: measure before optimizing

First collect a four-week operational baseline: source health, grouping review, original briefs produced, operator minutes, support requests, and cash costs. Reader measurement is optional and privacy-gated.

| Metric | Definition / limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Source-attribution completeness | published items with valid source/link/provenance ÷ sampled published items; target 100% |
| Merge precision | correctly grouped labeled same-event pairs ÷ all auto-merged labeled pairs; report sample and false merges |
| Publisher referral rate | measured outbound publisher clicks ÷ measured sessions/page views; name denominator; unavailable without instrumentation |
| Returning readers | agreed 7/28-day return definition using an approved method; do not infer from aggregate page views |
| Support conversion | completed provider-reported contributions ÷ an explicitly measured relevant exposure count; otherwise report raw totals only |
| Operator efficiency | minutes/source/month, minutes/original brief, maintenance incidents/week |
| Contribution | gross receipts − fees/tools/cash costs − operator hours × agreed rate |

Set audience/conversion targets after a baseline rather than promise arbitrary traffic. Search Console-style verified aggregate search reporting is a separate provider setup task; no guarantee of ranking or news-index inclusion.

## Stage 3: one reversible revenue experiment

All revenue mechanisms are disabled until current GitHub Pages commercial-use suitability is resolved in writing or hosting changes. GitHub's Pages limits say it is not intended/allowed as free hosting to run an online business or primarily facilitate commercial transactions. Do not infer approval for ads/donations from technical script support. Obtain an appropriate policy decision; if uncertain, move the commercial site to a suitable low-cost host while retaining the public code repository.

| Order | Experiment | Entry and stop gates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Voluntary hosted support/donations | Policy/provider/financial/privacy gates; quiet honest ask after value; stop if provider/compliance unresolved or UX harmed |
| 2 | Relevant disclosed affiliate commerce | Useful original release/deal/buying content, program acceptance and terms; stop on trust complaints or negative contribution |
| 3 | Direct digest/site sponsorship | Repeat audience and a truthful media kit; label placement, contract/invoicing privacy, no editorial control |
| 4 | Display advertising | Network eligibility/original-content approval, hosting/privacy/consent decision, reserved layout/script budget; stop if performance or referrals degrade |

The order is a hypothesis, not a commitment to implement every channel. Sponsorship may be preferable to ads for a small focused audience. Donation income is not assumed recurring. No bespoke payment backend or premium account system until proven demand justifies obligations.

### Experiment card

Each experiment records hypothesis, audience/exposure, approved data method, start/end, cash/time cap, expected benefit, performance/referral guardrails, disclosures, owner, and disable/rollback procedure. Review at four weeks; keep/change/stop with evidence. Do not A/B test monetization using hidden tracking introduced without a privacy gate.

### Financial scenarios, not forecasts

At a hypothetical $2/$8 page RPM, 10,000 monthly page views yield $20/$80 gross ad revenue; 100,000 yield $200/$800. These are illustrations, not expected results. Geography, provider eligibility, consent, ad blocking, traffic quality, seasonality, and placement matter. At small scale, thoughtful affiliates or one aligned sponsor can outperform banners, but require original value and commercial work.

Track domain/provider fees, taxes/accounting obligations where applicable, and operator hours. Zero hosting cost does not establish profitability. Two negative review periods trigger a stop/re-scope decision unless a consciously capped investment is recorded.

## Engagement guardrails

No paywall over publisher links, deceptive ads, infinite-scroll traps, forced signup, urgency gimmicks, notification nagging, or recurring-payment defaults. No lead-source ranking based on commission. Label sponsorship/affiliate relationships at the point of exposure. Keep core reading usable with JS disabled, ad blockers, or no tracking consent.

## Growth options, later

Public RSS/web digest first; consented email only when repeat demand and provider portability justify it. Release calendar, selected game pages, search, localization, community submissions, paid supporter features, and advanced grouping all need their own task/gate. Choose one measured next feature rather than increase maintenance surface indiscriminately.
