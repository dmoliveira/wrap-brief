# Provisional source-policy research — 2026-11-03

**Candidate research only: zero sources approved or enabled.** T02.02 and [issue #4](https://github.com/dmoliveira/wrap-brief/issues/4) remain blocked, including their T01.04 dependency. This note compares publicly available policy pages; it does not approve feeds, decide legal rights, authorize collection or establish a real-event benchmark. It is not legal advice.

The research consulted the policy pages linked below. No feed endpoint or publisher story was requested for this comparison, no publisher content was copied into the repository, and no publisher was contacted. Listed licenses and policies may change or contain item-specific exceptions; recheck them before relying on them. An attribution request, a public license or a usable feed is not alone a source-specific review of the complete proposed use.
