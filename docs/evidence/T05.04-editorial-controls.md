Caller-owned current-ledger selection and retention are mandatory. The existing CLI/legacy renderer remain synthetic component APIs, not an implicitly enforced editorial admission gate. No original brief text, private correspondence, arbitrary deletion engine, new dependency, provider or workflow is added.
