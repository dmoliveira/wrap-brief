## Bounded focus-ring repair and visual regression check — 2026-11-03

The prior shell checks did not measure every digest control's focus geometry, and document-wide overflow was not an individual text-clipping check. A new synthetic-browser probe observed **14 of 114** natural keyboard-focus states with an outline extending partly beyond the viewport across 18 fixture/viewport/root-text combinations. For example, the focused summary's bottom outline extended about 7px below a 320×900 viewport; an enlarged skip-link outline extended about 5px beyond a 320px viewport. One local synthetic screenshot confirmed the summary's bottom edge was cut while other outline edges remained visible. This was a concrete UX issue, **not by itself a finding of a WCAG failure or total focus invisibility**.

After separate design review, the shared stylesheet adds a 12px scroll margin to links and summaries and constrains the skip link to the viewport's available gutter width. These are best-effort scrolling and wrapping aids, not guarantees for oversized controls or nested scrollers. A new optional checker verifies the three synthetic goldens across the same 18 combinations, including **114 ordered keyboard targets** and summary focus both closed and open. Their computed outline geometry was contained in the tested viewport; computed minimum text contrast was **5.83:1**, and focused-text and outline contrast against the inferred ancestor surface were **6.24:1**. These are bounded computed-style/geometry results, not proof of painted pixels, absence of occlusion, all fonts or complete WCAG conformance.

The checker enumerates fixture text independently of visibility, checks text ranges and clipping ancestors, and rejects a defined set of unsupported effects instead of silently skipping them. Review found that root/body viewport overflow, text fill and masks, and a trailing keyboard control could previously evade the check; the guards were repaired and tested. Its **26 in-memory negative controls** cover those cases, outline contrast and geometry, hidden/clipped text, stylesheet checks, unexpected requests and an already-scrolled viewport; one positive control checks normal keyboard scrolling. The offline context fulfills only exact synthetic URLs; this is not an operating-system network sandbox. At 320×900 with 200% root text, a long unbroken synthetic skip label remained contained and reached `#main`; at 320×225 it was taller than the viewport, so full outline containment was **unassessed**, though Enter and subsequent Tab navigation worked. A classic-scrollbar configuration could not be exercised on this host.

With Playwright 1.62.1 and Chrome 156.0.8078.80, the existing digest/shell/reader/editorial browser checks also passed, including **12** and **18** separately intercepted synthetic source navigations. The current Python 3.13.14 gzip totals were **3403 / 3135 / 2845 bytes** for digest/corrected/empty. Three cold-loopback LCP observations were **415.93 / 419.96 / 416.04ms**, each with CLS **0** under the two-second lab profile above; they are not transfer-size or field-performance claims.

The scanner-enabled full suite passed **377 tests without skips on Python 3.13.14 and 3.11.15**; the 52 standalone planning tests, lock/Ruff checks, isolated wheel test and nine synthetic-artifact scan passed. After the last scrolling-control repair, the verifier repeated the updated visual checker twice and observed all 27 controls passing; focused re-review approved the repair.
