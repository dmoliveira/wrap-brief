# Corrections and removal requests

Scope: the **offline fictional prototype**, not an operating publisher service. There are no live publisher listings to request removal from today.

## Report an ordinary prototype error

For a non-sensitive error in a demonstration or documentation page, use the repository's [public issue route](https://github.com/dmoliveira/wrap-brief/issues/new/choose). Identify the page or synthetic example and briefly explain the error. Do not paste real publisher records, private correspondence, personal information, credentials or confidential rights claims. Issues, PRs and attachments are public.

## Confidential editorial or rights matters

**A confidential editorial/rights reporting channel has not yet been established.** Do not post a sensitive request publicly or use a vulnerability report as a substitute. An owner-approved appropriate route must exist before a live pilot. No email address, response deadline or identity-verification process is implied by this draft.

Suspected vulnerabilities or exposed credentials instead follow the separate private route in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md). If that security route is unavailable, do not fall back to a public disclosure.

## How reviewed remedies must work

A future approved listing must acknowledge material corrections without silently transferring a confirmation label to changed claims. Retractions must not become eligible leads again merely because an old feed or snapshot reappears. A reviewed removal must address applicable private state/exports/backups, public output, retained artifacts and owned caches; unresolved surfaces stay unresolved rather than being described as erased.

The current offline component demonstrates these controls on owned synthetic data. It is not a configured live service, an automatic request-handling process or a guarantee about provider retention or external copies. Removal records should retain only what is permitted and necessary; public notices must not repeat withdrawn content or expose private request details. We cannot promise deletion of third-party mirrors, forks or clones. See [source rights](sources.md) and [privacy](privacy.md).
