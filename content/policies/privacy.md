# Privacy

Scope reviewed: **2026-11-03, offline synthetic prototype**. This is not a privacy notice for an already deployed news service.

## The current prototype

The prototype has no reader accounts, analytics integration, advertising, affiliate measurement, newsletter signup, donation or payment integration. Its static reader does not set application tracking cookies, fingerprint readers or send analytics events. It uses local presentation assets rather than embedded third-party fonts, images, video or social widgets. No publisher feed is enabled.

This is a statement about the prototype application, **not** a claim that your browser, network, repository host or a linked website processes no data.

## GitHub and external links

Reading repository pages or submitting an issue/PR uses GitHub's service. GitHub may process account details, IP addresses, device/usage information and cookies under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Public issues, comments and attachments are public; do not put personal information, private correspondence, credentials or publisher requests there. Deleting a visible post does not guarantee removal from history, copies or third parties.

Following a source or other external link takes you to a third party under its own terms and privacy practices. The destination can receive ordinary request data; a publisher URL may retain query parameters. Wrap Brief does not promise tracking-free destination links or control those services' processing.

## Before any live pilot

No live-site hosting/logging configuration or confidential editorial contact has been established. Before launch, the operator must record the actual host, request/log data, purposes, access, retention/deletion and an appropriate contact/rights route, and update this notice for the intended audience and applicable requirements. No “zero IP collection,” retention period, consent exemption or legal-compliance guarantee is assumed.

Analytics, email, ads and payments remain disabled unless their separate review gates pass. A future material change requires updated disclosures before activation; it is not authorized by this draft. For current reporting limits, see [corrections and removal](corrections.md) and [security reporting](../../SECURITY.md).
