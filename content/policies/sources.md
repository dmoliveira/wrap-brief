# Sources, attribution and rights

**No live sources are currently approved or enabled.** All prototype stories are hand-authored fictional examples. A publicly readable feed, URL or headline is not by itself permission for every reuse, retention or commercial purpose.

## Before a publisher appears

Each source needs a reviewed basis covering the exact proposed use: permitted title/link/time fields, attribution, request frequency, private retention/deletion, public HTML/JSON/RSS, retained artifacts and owned caches, plus any commercial restrictions. Review records must remain current; private correspondence and contact details do not belong in this repository. Source availability and source permission are different questions.

The intended minimal listing identifies the publisher, preserves an approved title/link, labels unknown dates honestly and links to the original coverage. Alternate source records must remain attributable. Selecting a lead or applying a grouping override does not transfer authorship or establish confirmation. The current grouping component is not a certified real-news accuracy system.

No full article text, excerpts, publisher images, Nintendo artwork or automatic article rewriting is enabled. Any later use beyond the minimal approved fields requires its own rights review. No blanket legal conclusion about ordinary linking or all headline reuse is made here.

## Separate rights and remedies

Any eventual project code license would cover only its expressly authorized scope, **not publisher content, trademarks or third-party assets**. No project-specific code license has yet been adopted.

If reuse rights change or a reviewed correction/removal obligation applies, affected collection/output must be paused or restricted until resolved. Remedies must cover applicable private records/exports/backups, public output, retained artifacts and owned caches. A local synthetic drill does not prove hosted erasure, and we cannot promise deletion of external mirrors or clones. See [corrections and reporting limits](corrections.md) and the [current source investigation](../../docs/sources/FEASIBILITY.md).
