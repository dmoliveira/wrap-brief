# About Wrap Brief

**Nintendo news. Less repetition.** Wrap Brief aims to group coverage of the same event, show an attributed lead source and keep alternate perspectives available.

Today, this is an **offline prototype containing fictional demonstration stories**, not a live news site. No live publisher is approved or collected, no public pilot is operating, and there is no daily publishing promise. Demonstration titles and timestamps are not real news or evidence of freshness.

## Independent and unofficial

Wrap Brief is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo. Nintendo and other product names identify their respective owners' products; their use does not imply a partnership. The prototype uses locally authored presentation assets, not Nintendo logos, characters, screenshots or publisher images.

“Wrap Brief” is a working name. Brand/domain checks are not complete, and no trademark clearance or domain ownership is asserted.

## What a listing would mean

A future approved listing would link to its publisher and identify the source. A lead is a choice of coverage, not a claim that Wrap Brief originally reported or independently verified it. “Confirmed by source” and “Rumor — unconfirmed” are distinct labels; similarity alone cannot confirm a claim. Grouping quality is not yet qualified on a representative real-event benchmark. The prototype does not automatically rewrite articles or manufacture original reporting.
